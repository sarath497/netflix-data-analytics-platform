📖 Data Field Guide
🎬
MOVIE_TITLE
The full name of the movie including its release year in brackets (e.g. Inception (2010)). This is the unique identifier for each film in the dataset.
⭐
AVERAGE_RATING
The mean score a movie received from all its user reviews, on a scale of 0.5 to 5.0. A higher value means users consistently loved it. For example, a rating of 4.54 means nearly everyone gave it 5 stars. The overall dataset average is 3.45.
👥
TOTAL_RATINGS
The total count of individual user ratings submitted for that movie. A high number (e.g. 67,310 for Pulp Fiction) means the movie is widely watched and reviewed. This reflects a film's popularity, not necessarily its quality.
📊
What the data tells us
Together, these fields reveal two dimensions — quality (average rating) and reach (total ratings). A movie can be critically loved but little-known, or massively popular but average. The best movies score high on both.
